VPR V12 — LEGAL & COMPLIANCE EDITION

Brand retained:
Vehicle Pledge Registry (VPR)
Know. Verify. Lend with Confidence.
A member of Diranor Group.

V12 changes:
- Public result wording distinguishes information recorded on VPR from a legal declaration that a vehicle is unencumbered.
- Certificate is styled as a VPR Vehicle Interest Search Report.
- Multiple interests are described by Order of VPR Registration, not legal creditor priority.
- Terms of Use added.
- Privacy Policy added.
- Correction / Dispute process added.
- Public and certificate notices identify VPR as an independent private vehicle-interest information platform.
- VPR does not claim that recording alone creates/perfects statutory security or determines ownership/legal priority.

All V11 functionality is retained, including analytics, PDF/Excel traffic reports, backup/import, strict AAA1111 registration format, SQLite WAL, security protections, recovery and historical certificates.

Upgrade:
1. Download an Excel backup.
2. Back up data/vpr.sqlite.
3. KEEP your existing data/vpr.sqlite.
4. Replace the other files with V12.
5. Test public search, certificate, admin, Terms, Privacy and Correction pages.

The compliance wording reduces ambiguity but is not a substitute for Zimbabwean legal advice or regulatory registration/notification requirements.
